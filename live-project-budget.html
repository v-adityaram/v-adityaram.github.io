Budget - a daily expense tracker

Set a monthly spending budget, get a daily allowance, log expenses in two taps, and watch leftovers roll into tomorrow or straight into savings. Income, fixed commitments, bank balances, cards, and loans all live in one place.

Open Budget Back to Projects
Today

One number that matters

Your monthly spending budget is split across the days of the month. The Today screen shows what's left to spend right now, with a quick-add form for amount, tag, and note.

Rollover

Leftover goes where you want

Unspent money can carry to the next day or be swept into savings - a monthly default with a per-day override. Overspending always carries forward so the month stays honest.

Month

Income and commitments

Salary in, rent, family transfers, EMIs and SIPs out - with paid ticks. What's left after commitments funds your spending budget and planned savings. Plans copy forward each month.

Accounts

Net worth at a glance

Bank balances and cash on one side, credit card and loan outstandings on the other. Keep them manual, or turn on sync from a date you pick so expenses, paid commitments, received income, and transfers move money automatically.

Stack

How it's built

Cloudflare Worker serving a vanilla JS app and a JSON API on the same origin, with D1 (SQLite) for storage, PBKDF2 password hashing, HttpOnly session cookies, and invite-code gated sign-up.
