How the product fits together.

Today

One number that matters

Your monthly spending budget is split across the days of the month. The Today screen shows what's left to spend right now, with a quick-add form for amount, tag and note.

Favourites

Log the usual in one tap

Save the things you buy often — metro, chai, lunch — with or without a fixed amount. Tapping one fills the expense form instantly, ready to add or adjust.

Rollover

Leftover goes where you want

Unspent money can carry to the next day or be swept into savings — a monthly default with a per-day override. Overspending always carries forward so the month stays honest.

Month

Income and commitments

Salary in; rent, family transfers, EMIs and SIPs out, with paid ticks. What's left after commitments funds your spending budget and planned savings. Plans copy forward each month. Tap any tag in the spending-by-tag breakdown to see and edit every expense filed under it.

Accounts

Net worth at a glance

Bank balances and cash, credit card and loan outstandings, and locked assets like FDs and mutual funds that sit outside net worth until you toggle them in with a Liquid / With Locked switch.

Auto-sync

Real balances, on your terms

Keep balances manual, or turn on sync and pick default accounts — from then on, expenses, paid commitments, received income and transfers move real money automatically. Any single entry can still be pointed at a different account, or none. A reality-check panel compares the plan against your accounts and reconciles any gap with one tap.

Assistant

Tell it, don't type it

A built-in chat assistant answers questions about your budget and can add, edit or delete expenses and income/commitment items — by text or voice. It always proposes the change first and waits for a tap to confirm; nothing is written silently. Common shorthand ("200 uber hdfc") is parsed instantly with no AI call, with typo-tolerant matching against your real accounts and past tags; anything more open-ended goes to Gemini, which falls back to Cloudflare Workers AI automatically if it's ever unavailable.

How it's built

One Worker, one origin

A Cloudflare Worker serving a vanilla JS PWA and a JSON API from the same origin, with D1 (SQLite) for storage, PBKDF2 password hashing, HttpOnly session cookies, its own installable icon, invite-code gated sign-up, and a Gemini-backed assistant with an automatic Workers AI fallback.
