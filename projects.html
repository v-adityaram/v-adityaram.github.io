Ask about Aditya

A portfolio should answer follow-up questions. I built this assistant to answer from my published work, cite sources and say when the evidence is missing.

The corpus is small enough that a top-K cutoff turned out to be a liability — the first version dropped the one chunk a recruiter's question needed. It now gives the model the whole corpus and uses the vector scores to rank citations against the answer that was actually written, which is a more honest source trail than similarity to the question.
